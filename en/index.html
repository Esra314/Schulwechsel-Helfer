    Want to go to a Gymnasium? Here's how

    The grade total is above 14. If your child would like to go to a Gymnasium, the way there is the Probeunterricht (trial lessons). Registration is voluntary and goes through the primary school. German, maths and cross-subject skills are assessed. The deadlines are on the official page.

      Checklist for choosing a school

      • Foreign languages: Which languages are offered, and when does a second foreign language start?
      • All-day school: Are there lessons and activities into the afternoon?
      • Profile: Does the school have a focus, for example STEM (MINT), music, sports or bilingual classes?
      • Way to school: How long does it take on foot, by bike, by bus or train?
      • Lunch: Is there a canteen or a hot lunch?

      5 questions for the open day

      1. Which foreign languages are offered from grade 7, and when does the second foreign language start?
      2. What does a normal school day look like, and how long is it?
      3. Which focus areas, clubs and projects are there?
      4. How are children who need help supported, and how are strong children encouraged?
      5. How many applications were there last year, and how many places are there in grade 7?

          Next: About the school types

          Good to know: secondary schools in Berlin

          What is what? Short explanations of the school types, qualifications and terms you will meet when choosing a school.

          What is a Gymnasium?

          The Gymnasium leads to the Abitur in 6 years (grades 7 to 12). The pace is faster, and from grade 7 all children learn a second foreign language. There is no longer a trial year: suitability is decided beforehand, through the Notensumme or the Probeunterricht.

          What is an Integrierte Sekundarschule (ISS)?

          The ISS leads to all school-leaving qualifications: Berufsbildungsreife, Mittlerer Schulabschluss and Abitur. The Abitur is taken after grade 13, so with one more year than at a Gymnasium. Many ISS have their own upper school (Oberstufe) or work with one.

          What is a Gemeinschaftsschule?

          At a Gemeinschaftsschule all children learn together, often from grade 1 to 10 or up to the Abitur. Like the ISS, it leads to all qualifications, with the Abitur after grade 13.

          How does the Probeunterricht work?

          The Probeunterricht is for children who would like to go to a Gymnasium and have a grade total above 14. Registration is voluntary and goes through the primary school. On one day there are written tasks in German and maths, and cross-subject skills are looked at. Children who pass can register at a Gymnasium.

          Which school suits my child?

          Grades are not everything. Think about your child's learning pace, interests and strengths, the way to school, all-day programmes and languages. Visit open days and talk to your child's teacher.

          Can my child also get the Abitur at an ISS or Gemeinschaftsschule?

          Yes. Both school types lead to the Abitur, after grade 13. The extra year gives more time for learning, projects and practical work – many young people reach their best result this way.

          What does Ganztag (all-day school) mean?

          Gebundener Ganztag: lessons and activities go into the afternoon on several days, compulsory for all children. Teilgebundener Ganztag: compulsory only on some days. Offener Ganztag: afternoon activities are optional. Under “Details” in the school list you can see what each school offers.

          What do Profil, bilingual and Europa-Schule mean?

          A Profil is a focus of the school, for example music, sport, MINT (maths and science) or languages. Bilingual means some subjects are taught in a foreign language. At a Staatliche Europa-Schule Berlin children learn in two languages, for example German and Spanish.

          A grade is missing in the report, or it shows something else?

          For example an in-between grade like “2-3”, a different subject, or a report from another federal state or country. Special rules apply then. The primary school advises you and calculates the Förderprognose. This page cannot calculate such cases exactly.
